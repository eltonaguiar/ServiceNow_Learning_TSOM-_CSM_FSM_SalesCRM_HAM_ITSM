# Official ServiceNow Video Resources

To avoid outdated or inaccurate community tutorials, always start with official ServiceNow channels. Depending on your goal (building, documenting, or learning best practices), use the corresponding channel below.

## 📺 Official Channels

| Channel | Handle | Focus | Best For... |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ServiceNow Dev Program** | [@ServiceNowDevProgram](https://www.youtube.com/@ServiceNowDevProgram) | Developer tools, APIs, scripting, and practical guidance for creators. | **Building:** Learning how to use Script Includes, Flow Designer, and APIs. |
| **ServiceNow Documentation** | [@ServiceNowDocs](https://www.youtube.com/@ServiceNowDocs) | Official documentation walk-throughs, product features, and release updates. | **Referencing:** Understanding how a specific OOB feature is intended to work. |
| **ServiceNow Community** | [@ServiceNowCommunity](https://www.youtube.com/@ServiceNowCommunity) | Community events, best practices, live streams, and answers to common user questions. | **Optimizing:** Learning real-world implementation patterns and expert tips. |
| **Main ServiceNow Channel** | [@servicenow](https://www.youtube.com/@servicenow) | General company overviews, keynote presentations, and major product announcements. | **Orienting:** High-level product vision and major platform shifts. |

---

## 🛠️ Pro-Tip: How to Search Effectively
When searching for a specific module (like HRSD or TSOM), combine the module name with the channel handle to filter out non-official content:
- `site:youtube.com "@ServiceNowDevProgram" HRSD`
- `site:youtube.com "@ServiceNowDocs" TSOM`
